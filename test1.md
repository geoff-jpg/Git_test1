# Where to Find Maths & Numerical Puzzles

## Free Online Resources

- **Project Euler** - https://projecteuler.net  
  Challenging mathematical/programming problems, numbered series, great for all levels.

- **NRICH** - https://nrich.maths.org  
  Rich mathematical tasks and puzzles, from Cambridge. Good range of difficulty.

- **Math Puzzles** - https://www.mathpuzzle.com  
  Wide variety of numerical puzzles and recreational maths.

- **Brilliant.org** - https://brilliant.org  
  Interactive problems in maths and logic. Free tier available.

- **Cut the Knot** - https://www.cut-the-knot.org  
  Large archive of mathematical puzzles and problems with solutions.

## Books

- *The Moscow Puzzles* - Boris Kordemsky
- *Mathematical Puzzles and Diversions* - Martin Gardner
- *The Art of Problem Solving* series - various authors

## Competitions & Challenges

- **UKMT** (UK Mathematics Trust) - https://ukmt.org.uk  
  Past papers and challenges, Junior/Intermediate/Senior levels.

- **AMC/AIME** (USA) - https://artofproblemsolving.com  
  Archive of past competition problems.

## Apps

- **Mathway** - step-by-step problem solving
- **Photomath** - scan and solve
- **Elevate** - daily numerical brain training
